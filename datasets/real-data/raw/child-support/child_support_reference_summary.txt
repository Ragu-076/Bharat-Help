COLLECTION NOTES
- The official WCD page describes Child Helpline 1098, round-the-clock state/district services and integration with ERSS. The SOP is dated 31 March 2023; keep current service verification separate from the document's publication date.
- The SOP supplies initial child-protection assistance/referral information, including abuse, missing children, labour and some abandonment/trafficking situations. Detailed rights and current local contacts still require additional verification.
- NCPCR's eBaalNidan portal provides public Register Complaint and Track Complaint routes for child-rights complaints.
- The registration form requests complainant/contact and child information, location, complaint details and case-status declarations. It includes CAPTCHA and supporting-evidence options.
- The tracking form requests a registered email/mobile, complaint ID and CAPTCHA. Do not collect actual complaint credentials, identities or case files into the application dataset.
- CERT-In's Cyber Smart Kids: Suraksha Guide (October 2025) is an awareness reference for privacy, phishing, online relationships and safer internet use.

Retain source URLs and verification dates when turning these sources into reviewed assistance records. These reference files do not automatically update the running API.
