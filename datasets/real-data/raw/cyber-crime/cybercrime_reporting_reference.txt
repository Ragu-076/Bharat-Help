REPORTING REFERENCE
- The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal provides reporting routes for online fraud, hacking and other cybercrimes. Financial cyber fraud can also be reported through 1930, according to the MHA reference.
- Use the portal's relevant reporting category and current instructions. The special Women/Child route has a restricted content-related scope; anonymous reporting must not be offered as a universal option for harassment or other complaints.
- Prepare incident information and relevant evidence. Financial cases may require bank/wallet details, transaction references, dates and amounts.
- Keep reporting and tracking on the official portal. Bharat Help's public dataset should contain guidance and source links, without citizens' account details, identities, credentials or evidence files.

PAYMENT-SAFETY REFERENCE
- NPCI warns about fake cashback links, deceptive QR requests, unknown applications, fake investments, impersonation and threatening messages.
- Entering a UPI PIN authorises a payment; it is not required to receive money. Check the intended recipient before approving a payment.

Keep source URLs, geographic scope and verification dates with extracted records. The downloadable brochure has no publication date identified during checking.
